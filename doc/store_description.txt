CONFIGURE EVERYTHING FROM THE WATCH
Long-press the watch face to open the settings menu and change every option directly on your wrist. You can also use the Garmin Connect app if you prefer.

FEATURES
- Up to 8 data fields, each one independently configurable
- 17 data types: weather, calendar, notifications, sunrise/sunset, altitude, heart rate, barometer, battery, battery days, stress, Body Battery, steps, floors, calories, solar intensity, temperature
- Optional 4-hour sensor history graph: heart rate, barometer or altitude, in small or large size
- Tap any field to open the related Garmin app or complication
- Dark and light themes
- Battery Save mode: time and date only
- Status icons: Bluetooth, Do Not Disturb, alarm
- 24-hour military time option
- AMOLED always-on mode with burn-in protection
- Fast wake-up and low power consumption
- Optimized for every supported screen size
- Languages: English, Italian, Spanish
